# Ludicord Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want the Ludicord community to be welcoming, useful, and safe for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, location, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar terms.
- Respect privacy and never pressure someone to share private code, account
  data, credentials, or personal information.
- Give credit for ideas, examples, and prior work.
- Accept reasonable moderation decisions and corrections.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or sexualized attention.
- Personal insults, deliberate intimidation, or repeated disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing malicious code, credentials, tokens, or instructions intended to
  compromise another user or service.
- Spam, impersonation, or deceptive promotion.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, the
Ludicord Discord server, and other spaces where someone represents the
Ludicord community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not post a sensitive conduct report publicly. Contact a moderator in the
[Ludicord Discord server](https://discord.gg/FzvXfvyPVe) and provide only the
information needed to investigate. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
limit participation, or ban accounts when behavior threatens the community.

Moderators will consider context, severity, repetition, and impact. Good-faith
reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible. Retaliation
against a reporter or participant is not accepted.
